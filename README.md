# jazzalyn_tim_wedding
Jazzalyn &amp; Tim's Wedding Website Demo

Demo Website: https://softqueerwealth-png.github.io/jazzalyn-tim-wedding/

The demo is a working, mobile-first wedding site for Jazzalyn & Tim. It inspired and reworks The Mahogany Pages' city guide so that it is organized by wedding phase.
It was built from screenshot inspiration of staging.softqueerwealth.com, not its code. The TMP codebase stays private, and an engineer can port these patterns into it. It is one self-contained HTML file with no build step. Content lives in a data block at the top of the script, and layout is kept separate.

Demo walkthrough for Jazzalyn
Allow about 10 minutes. Start with how the site feels, then show how it works. Present on a phone: most guests will see it there. Tap Reset demo in the top bar before you start.
1. Home: “this is yours.” Open the hero. Point out her name and Tim's, Los Angeles, and “Date to be announced.” Explain that the photo frames are placeholders for their approved photos.
2. Our Love Story. Scroll through the chapters: how we met, our journey, the proposal, favorite memories and a message to guests. Tap Edit our story, type a line and save it. This shows she can write it herself.
3. The Journey cards. Five chapters across three cities. Make the key point here: the wedding isn't one day, it's a collection of celebrations.
4. Our Wedding (the guest view). Date, times, venues, dress code, transport and RSVP each have a clear slot. Unconfirmed details say “To be announced” instead of being guessed.
5. Explore → Bachelorette. Choose the celebration and show the four-day New Orleans itinerary grouped by day. It works like the city list on TMP today.
6. Filters: Bachelorette + June. Open Filters, pick the phase and the month, and the button shows the result count live. Then show Reset filters.
7. Know Before You Go. Tap it on the Welcome Dinner to show attire, cost and the reservation. Then open the KBYG tab for the New Orleans city guide and the interactive Before You Go checklist.
8. Add + My Plan. Add two events, open My Plan and tap Copy my plan for the group chat.
9. LA Places. Choose Wedding Weekend → Places, then filter by Stay. Show neighborhood, price, distance to the venues and the group-block field.
10. Timeline → Calendar. Show February and June. Point out that “Dates to be announced” collects everything that isn't set yet.
11. Meet the Wedding Party. Editorial profile cards with strengths. She decides what's public.
12. Dashboard: the practical value. Show the Demo Mode banner first, then the overview: outstanding and overdue counts, needs attention, responsibilities by person. Switch Viewing as to Simone to show what a bridesmaid sees. Change a task to Completed.
13. Add a task with a suggestion. Tap + Add task and pick “Budget management.” The site suggests Kendra and shows how many open tasks she has.
14. Privacy. Turn Demo Mode off from the menu. The dashboard link disappears and the page asks for sign-in.
Close by asking her for three things: approved photos, their story in their own words, and the real wedding-party list with roles.
